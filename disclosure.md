The fiction on this site is written by a human. The world, the characters, the story — all human.

AI (Claude) assists with:

- **Site development** — building and maintaining the site: layout, styling, infrastructure.
- **Editing** — spelling, grammar, and formatting corrections to the text I write.
- **Worldbuilding suggestions** — occasionally, AI may suggest an addition to the narrative. These are always marked with a <span class="ai-addition" title="Added by AI">dotted underline</span>. Hover over any underlined passage (or long-press on mobile) to see a tooltip confirming it was added by AI.

If it's not underlined, a human wrote it.
